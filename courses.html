My Enrolled Courses

Your actual courses, harvested from your own history — deduped against the Interstitium catalog (global rule: no duplicated courses), tracked with resume links. Everything stays in your browser.

Import from history

  1. In Chrome: History → History → ⋮ → Export (or copy rows from chrome://history). Phone history and dormant tabs count — paste them too.
  2. Paste the export below — URLs and page titles. Nothing leaves your browser; parsing is local.
  3. We extract course-like entries, dedupe against the catalog by URL and title similarity, and save the track to localStorage.
