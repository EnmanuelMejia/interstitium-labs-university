The Packing Playground

Eight live parameters drive an original seeded circle-packing engine written for this course. There is no animation loop — every render is a deliberate, reproducible study. Change one axis, keep the seed, watch the field reorganize.

Parameters

28

Minimum gap enforced between circles. Low: foam. High: constellation.

15

How steeply radius shrinks across placement order. 0: monumental. 100: cascade.

3

Number of violet-family hues cycling through the field.

255

Stroke opacity. Lower values let overlaps blend into depth.

1.1

Horizontal component of the post-packing rotation.

1.2

Vertical component. Large values crop hard against the frame.

The arithmetic of overlap. Highest-leverage single control.

seed 7

Same seed + same parameters = same field, forever. That is the whole method.

Guided studies

Study 1 — The foam (Span low)

Set Span to 8, keep everything else at default, keep the seed. Watch how dense the field gets before the attempts budget gives up. Note the seed and the circle count in your notebook: that count is the honest density ceiling for these settings.

Study 2 — The constellation (Span high, Alpha down, lighter)

Span 70, Alpha 140, BLEND on lighter, same seed. Test whether sparse marks with glowing intersections can carry a composition — or whether the field falls apart without density.

Study 3 — The tilt (angles past 2.0)

Push AngleX past 2.0 and AngleY past −2.0. Watch circles crop against the frame. Decide: does the crop imply a larger field, or does it just read as damage? There is no wrong answer — there is only your notebook entry.
